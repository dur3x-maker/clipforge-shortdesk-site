Using ClipForge ShortDesk

These terms apply to the ClipForge ShortDesk desktop tool and its informational website. ShortDesk prepares local MP4 clips and their metadata, manages publishing queues, and sends eligible uploads to connected platforms when you enable and confirm real publishing.

Your accounts and content

Use only accounts you own or are authorized to manage. You are responsible for having the rights, permissions, and consents needed for your videos, gameplay footage, audio, captions, and any other material you submit. Follow applicable law and the rules of each destination platform. Review the selected account, content, and visibility settings before confirming an upload.

You retain your rights in your content. By requesting an upload, you authorize ShortDesk to transmit the selected video and its publishing details to the destination platform on your behalf.

Connected platforms and automation

ShortDesk uses official platform interfaces for its implemented integrations. Platform authorization, account eligibility, quotas, privacy settings, moderation, and API restrictions can affect delivery. ShortDesk offers no TikTok connection or publishing at this time.

A queue entry is a plan. Real upload commands require explicit confirmation. You can stop pending uploads by stopping scheduled ShortDesk runs and withholding new upload confirmations. Instagram also has a setting to disable writes. An upload already sent to a platform may still be processed there.

Disconnecting an account

Stop real runs for the platform, revoke access or disable the token through the platform, and remove the local credential copies and related settings. Existing posts are managed on the destination platform. See the Privacy Policy for local deletion steps.

Platform outages, permission changes, and rate limits may delay or prevent uploads. Any rights you have under applicable law continue to apply.
