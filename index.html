ClipForge ShortDesk 2.0

Manage your short gaming videos.

ClipForge ShortDesk is a desktop tool for preparing and managing short gaming video publications. It takes finished MP4 clips from your local workflow and helps you plan what goes out, where, and when.

How it works

Start with local MP4s

Use finished clips from your local workflow. Prepare titles, descriptions, and hashtags for each post.

Manage the queue

Plan publishing slots and check delivery status. Local publication records help prevent duplicate uploads.

Connect accounts

ShortDesk uses Google authorization for YouTube and a Meta-issued access token for Instagram. Platform requests go through official APIs.

Control publishing

Use a dry run to plan a queue. Real uploads require explicit confirmation in ShortDesk. Instagram writes can also be disabled in settings.

Local records

Queue records and publication history are kept in SQLite on your device. Credentials are stored in separate local files. The Privacy Policy explains what is sent to connected platforms and how to remove local data.

TikTok integration

TikTok support is planned. ShortDesk currently offers no TikTok account connection or publishing through the Content Posting API.
